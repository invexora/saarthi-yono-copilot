02 / Strategic Rationale
The Engagement & Cross-Sell Opportunity
Traditional digital banking relies on reactive menus and broadcast SMS blasts. Saarthi unlocks high-converting contextual engagement.
Current Industry & App Reality

📉 1.5% Cross-Sell Conversion

Generic popups, banners, and cold SMS are ignored as spam. Customer intent is missed when timing does not align with financial cash flows.

⏳ Massive Branch Operational Load

Customers visit physical branches for routine queries (KCC reviews, Life Certificates, Tax challans, KYC) that YONO could resolve in 2 clicks.

💸 CASA Outflow to FinTechs & Private Banks

Idle salary balances sit earning low interest, encouraging tech-savvy customers to move deposits to third-party investment apps.

The Saarthi Proactive Advantage

🚀 12%–18% Proactive Conversion

Interventions are presented at the exact moment of financial relevance (e.g. salary jump, high card spend, property search, foreign trip booking).

🏦 Autonomous CASA Retention (Auto-Sweep & SIP)

Keeps customer liquidity inside SBI by automatically suggesting MODS Auto-Sweep and SBI Mutual Fund SIPs before money leaves the bank.

🛡️ Built-In Distress & Scam Guardian

Automatically blocks cross-sells for vulnerable users; provides 1-tap card freeze and connects to RM when financial distress is detected.

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Separation of duties between System Operators, Compliance Auditors, and Credit Underwriters.
